## Interview prep (Laravel)

Be ready to explain on a whiteboard or call:

1. Request lifecycle in Laravel (route → middleware → controller/Livewire → Eloquent → response)  
2. How you made **payment/logistics webhooks idempotent**  
3. **Queue** design for PDF jobs (why Redis, failed jobs, workers/systemd)  
4. Auth: Fortify / Socialite / 2FA choices  
5. Deploy: Nginx + PHP-FPM + env + migrations + zero-downtime idea  
6. Walk through **one live app** for 5 minutes (GatewayHub or DocuTrust)
